# OpenPE

Landing page for the OpenPE desktop studio.

Apache-2.0. English by default, with a Chinese toggle.

Commands and copy limits are in AGENTS.md. Inter and JetBrains Mono are licensed under the SIL Open Font License.

## Demo media

The site uses the 98-second OpenPE EMC Flyback workflow film. The homepage player uses a board-only poster and a fast-start H.264 encode of the current master with the original AAC track. The clips and stills are generated by `scripts/cut-clips.sh`. The Agents section presents a simplified version of the film's Overall diagram, with the Agent and GUI paths through the Uniform API to Core and plugins. The thermal card shows the PCB temperature field. The Layout Parasitics card shows the DRV route from U1 pad 6 through a via to RG pad 1 and its copper-layer cross-section.

The soundtrack is “Tech House vibes” by Alejandro Magaña (A. M.), Mixkit #130. [Mixkit Stock Music Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree/)

## Team artwork

Natural Earth land data guided the geographic distribution of circuit traces. The university nodes use latitude and longitude projected into the map. The SVG has a transparent background and records the source data in its metadata. The credits follow the OpenPE film order.

University identifiers use the supplied Berkeley seal, the UCLA campus wordmark, the MIT block mark, the Glasgow coat of arms isolated from the university's unboxed logo, the TU/e header mark and the Westlake University visual identity mark. The Team map renders them in the OpenPE green palette, with tonal detail for the two crests.
