# OpenPE

Landing page for the OpenPE desktop studio.

Apache-2.0. English by default, with a Chinese toggle.

Commands and copy limits are in AGENTS.md. Inter and JetBrains Mono are licensed under the SIL Open Font License.

## Demo media

The site uses the 98-second OpenPE EMC Flyback workflow film. The homepage player uses a board-only poster and a fast-start H.264 encode of the current master with the original AAC track. The clips and stills are generated by `scripts/cut-clips.sh`. The thermal card shows the PCB temperature field. The Layout Parasitics card shows the DRV route from U1 pad 6 through a via to RG pad 1 and its copper-layer cross-section.

The soundtrack is “Tech House vibes” by Alejandro Magaña (A. M.), Mixkit #130. [Mixkit Stock Music Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree/)

## Team artwork

Natural Earth land data guided the geographic distribution of circuit traces. The university nodes use latitude and longitude projected into the map. The SVG has a transparent background and records the source data in its metadata. Academic links cover appointments, visiting roles and education. The credits follow the OpenPE film order. Select a school to see the relevant academic connections.

University identifiers use the [MIT block mark](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MIT_2023_red_logo.svg), the [UCLA campus wordmark](https://www.ucla.edu/img/logo-ucla.svg), the [UC Berkeley favicon](https://brand.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/favicon.png), the [TU/e header mark](https://www.tue.nl/typo3conf/ext/tue_menu/Resources/Public/Sprite/sprite.35ce45cd45.svg) and the [Westlake University visual identity package](https://en.westlake.edu.cn/about/VIS/).
