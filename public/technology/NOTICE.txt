Source: https://github.com/Open-Cascade-SAS/OCCT/blob/V7_8_1/dox/resources/occt_logo.png

ngspice header logo (nglogo.jpg)
Source: https://ngspice.sourceforge.io/images/nglogo.jpg
Credit: ngspice project. Converted to PNG without changing decoded pixels.
ngspice-mono.png retains the lettering and a disc outline with transparency.

Gmsh icon (utils/icons/gmsh-no-text.png)
Source: https://gmsh.info/src/gmsh-4.15.2-source.tgz
Credit: Gmsh project. Resized proportionally for the homepage.
gmsh-mono.png trims the transparent margins and converts the facets to one color.

Elmer icon (pics/ElmerLogoPlain64x64.png)
Source: https://github.com/ElmerCSC/elmerfem/releases/download/release-26.1/elmerfem-release-26.1.tar.gz
Credit: Elmer FEM project. The original PNG is preserved as published.
elmer-mono.png converts the colored mark to a single-color mask with transparency.
